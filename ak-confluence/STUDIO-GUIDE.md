# Studio Guide — AK Confluence

This is a plain-English guide to editing the website. You do not need to understand Sanity.
The Studio is organized by what you're trying to change. Everything you edit here shows up on
**www.akconfluence.com** after you press **Publish**.

## How to add or change things

### Add a counselor
1. Go to **Our Team → Counselors** and click the **+** (Create) button.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Title / License** (e.g. "Licensed Professional Counselor"), and **Photo**.
3. Set **Availability** (Open / Full / Waitlist / Unavailable).
4. Turn on **Founder** only if they're the founder.
5. Pick their **Concerns** and **Services** from the lists.
6. Add a **Booking link** (their intake/booking URL) if they take clients.
7. Set **Display order** (lower number = earlier in the list).
8. Press **Publish**.

### Add a FAQ
1. Go to **What We Offer → FAQs** and click **+**.
2. Type the **Question** and the **Answer** (use the formatting buttons for bold/links).
3. Set **Display order** (lower = higher on the page).
4. Press **Publish**.

### Add a provider endorsement
1. Go to **Our Team → Provider Endorsements** and click **+**.
2. Paste the **Quote** and the provider's **Name**.
3. Add **Credentials** (e.g. "PhD") and **Organization** if applicable.
4. Turn on **Feature on website** so it shows on the home page.
5. Press **Publish**.

### Add an article (newsletter post)
1. Go to **Articles & Newsletter → Articles** and click **+**.
2. Fill in **Title**, **Publish date**, **Author**, **Excerpt**, and the **Body**.
3. Click **Generate** next to the slug to create the web address.
4. Press **Publish**.

## How to publish (make changes live)

1. Make your changes — they are saved as a **draft** automatically.
2. Click the green **Publish** button in the bottom-right corner.
3. The change goes live on the website within a minute or two.

> If you see "Discard changes", that undoes the draft. "Publish" is what makes it live.

## Notes

- **Book Now** is a single place: change it once in **Navigation & Footer** and every "Book Now" button on the site updates.
- **Concerns** power both the "Find the words that fit" page *and* the counselor filter chips. Turn on **"Show as a filter on the Counselors page"** for a concern to appear as a chip.
- A counselor's **Availability** and **Show on website** are separate: "Unavailable" still shows the person, while "Show on website" off hides them entirely.
