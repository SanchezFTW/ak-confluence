# AK Confluence — Therapy & Counseling in Anchorage, Alaska

## About
AK Confluence (Confluence LLC) is a collaborative therapy practice based in Anchorage, Alaska. The practice offers individual therapy, couples counseling, trauma recovery, somatic work, and family sessions. Virtual sessions are available statewide across Alaska.
