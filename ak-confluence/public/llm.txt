# AK Confluence — Therapy & Counseling in Anchorage, Alaska

## About
AK Confluence (Confluence LLC) is a collaborative therapy practice based in Anchorage, Alaska. The practice offers individual therapy, couples counseling, trauma recovery, somatic work, and family sessions. Virtual sessions are available statewide across Alaska.

## Specialties
Anxiety, Depression, Couples Therapy, Grief and Loss, Somatic Therapy, Trauma, Family Therapy, EMDR, Brainspotting

## Therapeutic Approaches
Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing), Brainspotting, Accelerated Resolution Therapy, Gottman Method (couples), breathwork, family systems theory, and person-centered counseling.

## Insurance
Most private health plans accepted; coverage is typically verified within the first week of consultation. Out-of-pocket options are available. No sliding-fee scale at this time.

## Location
Anchorage, Alaska. In-person sessions in Anchorage and virtual (HIPAA-compliant telehealth) sessions available throughout Alaska.
